Under the glass · mathematics and controls

The path introduces lenses once, then leaves their buttons in the corners and their parameters beside the instrument. Revisiting a stop does not overwrite your combination. Orbits → All controls reveals everything immediately. The animated Euler tape stays above the panels.

N accepts fractional frontiers from 1 to 5000. Moving it pauses and rebuilds the sieve, prime powers, ledger and tape at that point; no historical notes are sounded. Set A and B, then Replay to run from A, or Loop A–B to repeat that interval. Looping preserves the overshoot of each frame. Reset returns to 1 and disables the loop without changing your lenses or exponent. Seeking restarts decorative gap-disk angles and sound trails. Loop repeats N; Spin, if enabled, continues sweeping τ.

Start the beat. Every panel moves continuously at 1/p of its speed; a panel of width 1/p covers noon during the multiples of p. Glass adds the p², p³, … echoes. The mechanical panel footprints stay fixed when you tune the complex weights.

Weights adds q⁻ˢ = q⁻σ exp(−iτ log q) at the moving panels. Sum places the integer weights end to end, with real values pointing toward noon. During each beat the next arrow grows continuously; at integer x the endpoint is exactly the finite sum through x. The chain and the panel weights use separate, fixed display scales.

Continue brings in the coral vector x¹⁻ˢ/(s−1). At integer frontiers, the sum plus this vector tends to ζ(s) for σ > 0, s ≠ 1. The gold reference is an Euler–Maclaurin estimate with six correction terms; the tether is the remaining finite-frontier discrepancy. The pole at s = 1 is not a finite arrow. The gold reference and the sum may leave the viewport; they are never clamped to the rim.

First zero tunes σ and τ smoothly to ½ and 14.1347251417. The corrected endpoint approaches the central gold reference as the counting frontier grows. The ordinary sum does not converge there. Staircase independently adds the zero-frequency chain while the mechanism continues; Echoes admits more zero pairs gradually.

Every prime-power birth adds log p to ψ(x). Its link to zeta is −ζ′(s)/ζ(s) = Σ (log p)p⁻ᵏˢ for Re(s) > 1. Inverting this transform gives ψ₀(x) = x − Σρ x^ρ/ρ − log(2π) − ½log(1−x⁻²). The zero sum uses symmetric limits and gives the midpoint at jumps. The displayed finite zero sum is approximate and rings near jumps. Its normalized vectors are −2xⁱᵞ/(½+iγ); only their real projection is compared with the ledger. The supplied 30 zero heights are not derived by this finite machine. The Riemann hypothesis remains unproved.

Turn varies the exponent, not counting time. Spin sweeps Turn while the machine runs; stop it to hold the phase. All lenses and tuning changes ease continuously. The white beat never changes clocks. Space pauses or resumes; reset restarts the count without changing your tuning. Product retains the original finite sieve-density spiral at exponent 1.
